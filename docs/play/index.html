The claim

One hop at a time

A hop names who cited whom. Mark the last one as the origin if that is where it starts.

The chain

Three numbers are shown, and they differ: citations, repetitions, and independent sources. Ten outlets quoting one blog is ten citations and one independent source.

    The passage

    Paste what you were sent. Each flag names the technique and quotes the sentence, so you can disagree with the flag rather than trust it. It never says the passage is true or false, and never says the writer is good or bad.
